I was nervous the first time I cooked for someone I wanted to impress. I'd chosen pasta with cream sauce, which seemed bold. I prepped everything hours before they arrived and ran through the steps over and over until I'd memorized each motion and couldn't mess it up even if I tried. Maybe three times. My hands shook when I started cooking, but I focused. The smell of garlic and butter filled the kitchen. Suddenly I wasn't nervous anymore. When they took the first bite and their eyes lit up, all of it felt worth it. That's when I learned what cooking is. Showing someone you care.
