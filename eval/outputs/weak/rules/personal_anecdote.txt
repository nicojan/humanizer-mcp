I made lasagna for the first time because I thought I had to impress her. I'd seen the recipe. Layers, sauce, pasta. Easy, right? Wrong. I bought the wrong ricotta. Mixed it wrong. By the time it came out of the oven, the whole thing had separated. Watery. The noodles were somehow crunchy. I almost threw it away, but she was already there, and I was too embarrassed. We sat at the table. I braced. She took a bite, waited, took another. Then she laughed. Not at the food. At me, sitting there waiting for judgment. The lasagna was bad. But it meant something because I'd tried. That matters more than I thought it would. That's what she said. We ordered pizza and ate my disaster anyway.
