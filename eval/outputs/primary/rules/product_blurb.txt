This bottle was built for the miles between trailheads, not for a desk. The 32 oz brushed stainless steel body keeps water cold for up to 24 hours — long enough that you'll be back at the car before it warms. It drops in a backpack side pocket, handles a dropped pack without denting, and the leak-proof lid doesn't require the three-step ritual some designs do.

No plastic liner. No metallic taste. Wide enough to fill from a stream with a filter, narrow enough to fit a standard cup holder when you're driving to the trailhead.

It's heavy enough to feel substantial and light enough that you'll actually bring it. A few hikers in our test group switched away from insulated plastic after their first long day with this one. The two-finger carry loop is a small thing, but you'll miss it if it's not there.

One bottle. Fills from anywhere, keeps it cold, doesn't quit. That's what matters out there.
