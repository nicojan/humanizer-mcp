Your pack is already heavy enough without a bottle that quits on you halfway up. The Summit 750 is built from 18/8 food-grade stainless steel — no plastic liner, no metallic taste, no worries about what's leaching into your water after a few hundred miles.

Double-wall vacuum insulation keeps cold drinks cold for 24 hours and hot drinks hot for 12. The wide mouth fits ice cubes, a hydration tablet, or just makes end-of-day cleaning easy. The leak-proof lid locks shut with a quarter-turn, so you can toss it in your pack without thinking about it.

At 750ml, it hits the sweet spot between weight and capacity. It's also dishwasher safe, and the powder-coat finish holds up to the kind of scrapes and drops that tend to happen when you're more focused on the trail than your gear.
