There's a reason wool has kept people warm for a few thousand years, and this vest is a quiet argument for sticking with what works. It's knit from a dense, springy wool that traps heat without the bulk, so you get the warmth of a thick layer in something you can wear under a shell or on its own. The fibers breathe as you move, wicking moisture and shedding the clammy overheated feeling that sinks synthetic layers.

Cut for real movement, with armholes that don't bind when you reach. The natural crimp of the wool helps it resist odor and bounce back into shape after a long day. This is the layer you'll reach for on cold mornings and keep reaching for, year after year.
