Pack it, drop it, drag it through a downpour, and it shrugs the whole thing off. This 60-liter duffel is cut from heavyweight coated fabric with welded, weather-sealed seams, so a sudden storm on the tarmac or a wet boat deck won't reach the gear inside. The base is reinforced where bags take the most abuse, and the zippers are the chunky, gloved-hand kind that won't jam when you're cold and in a hurry.

Sixty liters swallows a week of clothes and still has room for boots. Padded backpack straps tuck away when you'd rather carry it by the grab handles, and a separate ventilated end pocket keeps muddy or damp items away from everything else. Whether it's riding in a truck bed or checked under a plane, this is the bag you stop worrying about.
