My brother lives four time zones away now. I see him at Christmas, maybe once in the summer if the flights line up. Add it up and we're talking eight, ten days a year. We're both in our thirties. Be generous and say we each get another forty years. That's four hundred days. Less than a year and a half of his company, total, spread across the rest of my life.

I did this math one night and couldn't shake it. The people I assumed were permanent fixtures turn out to be on a countdown I never noticed starting. Friends scatter, parents age, everyone has their own gravity now. I'm not telling you this to be morbid. I'm telling you because I booked a flight the next morning.
