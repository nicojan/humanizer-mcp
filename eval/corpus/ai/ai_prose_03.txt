Ask anyone how they're doing lately and you'll get the same answer. Busy. So busy. Slammed, swamped, underwater. We say it with a sigh, but listen closely and there's something else underneath, a little flicker of pride dressed up as exhaustion.

Being busy has quietly become the thing we brag about. Nobody wants to admit they had a slow week, that they read a novel on the couch and went to bed early. That sounds like failure now, like you're not in demand. So we inflate our calendars and complain about them in the same breath, and everyone nods along because they're doing it too.

I caught myself doing it last week. Someone asked how I was, and before I'd even thought about it, out came the word. Busy. I wasn't, particularly. I just didn't know how else to sound like I mattered.
